MOCK TOWNSHIP BY-LAWS — DEMO ONLY
This is a stripped-down mock, NOT the real Sime Darby by-laws. It loosely
mimics themes from Sime Darby Property townships for hackathon testing.
Verify every rule with the actual township management before relying on it.

1. Property use
- Lots are residential unless gazetted for commercial use.
- Home businesses need written management approval and must not cause signage,
  parking, noise or delivery nuisance.
- Short-term rentals (e.g. Airbnb) need approval and neighbour notice; max 6 guests.

2. Renovation & extensions
- Submit plans before work. No structural, facade or boundary changes without consent.
- Work hours: Mon–Sat 08:00–18:00. No work on Sundays or public holidays.
- A contractor deposit and debris removal may apply.

3. Parking
- Park only in allotted bays. No parking on grass, roads or common areas.
- Commercial vehicles over 3 tonnes and trailers may not park overnight.
- Obstructive or repeat offences may be clamped or fined.

4. Pets
- Dogs must be leashed and under control in common areas; clean up immediately.
- No livestock or exotic animals. Maximum 2 dogs per household unless approved.
- Persistent barking or noise is a nuisance offence.

5. Waste & noise
- Bag refuse and place it in bins on collection day; no dumping in common areas.
- Quiet hours: 22:00–07:00. Avoid loud music, machinery or renovation then.

6. Common areas & facilities
- Do not obstruct walkways, corridors, drains or fire access.
- Use facilities per posted rules; bookings are first-come, first-served.
- Damage to common property is charged to the responsible household.

7. Signage & advertising
- No banners, posters or for-sale signs beyond the approved size and placement.
- Do not nail signs to trees, walls or common property.

8. Trees & landscaping
- Do not cut or remove trees or alter landscaping without approval.
- Maintain your lot's grass, drains and boundary hedges.

9. Fees & enforcement
- Management may issue reminders, fines or restrict facility access for breaches.
- Mock fines: RM100 parking/waste, RM250 unapproved renovation work, RM500
  repeat or nuisance offences. Appeals within 14 days.
- By-laws may change; the latest notice on the community board applies.

Contact your township management office for formal advice.
